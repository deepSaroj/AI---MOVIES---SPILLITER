Privacy Policy

We store your account details (username, email, hashed password) and job metadata (file names, durations, statuses) so the service works. Video files themselves are stored on the server's file storage — never inside the database — and are visible only to your account.

Uploaded and generated files are kept for the server's configured retention window (default 7 days), after which they may be deleted. You can delete a job and all of its files at any time from your dashboard.

We do not sell your data. Videos you process must be your own or otherwise authorized; do not upload content you have no right to use.
